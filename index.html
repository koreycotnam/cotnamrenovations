How it works

What to expect, from first call to final walkthrough

  1. Tell Korey about it

    Send a few details through the form, or call. Korey gets back to you within two business days.

  2. Consult visit

    Korey comes to see the space, measures up, and talks through what you want and what's possible.

  3. Honest hourly pricing

    Korey charges by the hour. He'll give you a general sense of how long the job should take, then work efficiently so you only pay for the time it needs.

  4. Build and walkthrough

    Regular updates while the work is underway, a tidy jobsite each day, and a final walkthrough together.

Meet Korey

A local renovator who cares for your home because it matters

Korey lives and works on the Sunshine Coast. He takes on a small number of projects at a time so he can do the work himself and do it properly.

Because he handles nearly everything on his own, bringing in licensed electricians and plumbers only when needed, there's no waiting around on a string of subcontractors. He works to finish your project efficiently, so your home and daily routine are back to normal as soon as possible.

  • A general time estimate up front, with updates if things change once walls are opened or materials arrive
  • A tidy jobsite at the end of each day
  • Regular updates, so you always know what's next
  • Local references available on request
